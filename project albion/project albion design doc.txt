# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Core Concept

Develop a top-down RPG world consisting of four distinct civilizations:

1. Albion
2. Pictland
3. Gaetland
4. Ivernia

Each civilization possesses its own settlements, geography, points of interest (POIs), environmental features, and regional enemy types.

The world should be designed as a persistent, interconnected environment with exploration as a primary gameplay activity.

## 1.2 Design Requirements

- Top-down camera perspective.
- Fully editable world and level layouts.
- Persistent world containing settlements and exploration locations.
- Distinct geographical identities for each civilization.
- Caves, underground locations, rivers, lakes, coastlines, and diving locations.
- Exploration-oriented POI placement.
- Region-specific enemy populations.
- Compatibility with the selected development kits.

## 4.1 Enemy Distribution Requirements

- Each civilization must have its own regional enemy population.
- Enemy placement should correspond to geographical features and POIs.
- Enemy encounters should be distributed throughout the world, not restricted to settlements.
- Certain enemies may occupy caves, ruins, forests, mountains, burial sites, and other appropriate locations.
- Cross-regional enemy placement is not assumed and requires separate design approval.

# 5. EXPLORATION AND ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

## 5.1 Terrestrial Exploration

The world must support exploration of:

- Settlements.
- Ruins.
- Forests.
- Mountains.
- Burial mounds.
- Abandoned structures.
- Roads and crossings.
- Military encampments.
- Hidden locations.

## 5.2 Cave System

Caves are a distinct category of explorable geographical locations.

Required functionality:

- Cave entrances integrated into the world map.
- Traversable cave interiors.
- Connection between cave entrances and their corresponding interiors.
- Underground POIs and encounters.
- Potential underground water systems.

## 5.3 Water and Diving System

Water must be represented as an explorable part of the environment.

Required geographical elements:

- Lakes.
- Rivers.
- Coastal waters.
- Marshes.
- Flooded caves.
- Submerged ruins and shipwrecks.

Diving locations must be identifiable and accessible through the world environment.

**Functional requirement:** Water exploration and underwater traversal must be treated as separate requirements from ordinary land movement. The implementation of swimming, diving, underwater interaction, and underwater combat remains to be specified.

# 6. WORLD EDITING REQUIREMENTS

The world must remain editable throughout development.

Required editing capabilities:

1. Add, remove, and reposition settlements.
2. Add, remove, and reposition POIs.
3. Modify geographical terrain.
4. Modify water bodies and coastlines.
5. Add and modify cave entrances.
6. Edit cave interiors.
7. Place and modify enemy populations.
8. Modify roads and connections between settlements.
9. Modify the geographical boundaries of civilizations.

The world should use a modular layout that permits individual regions and locations to be modified without requiring reconstruction of the entire world.

# 7. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

## 7.1 Development Kits

The project currently identifies two development kits:

| Kit | Intended Project Role |
|---|---|
| Tiny Toony RTS Kit | Candidate environment, terrain, and world-building framework. |
| Moore's RPG Template | Candidate RPG gameplay and character-system framework. |

The exact division of functionality between the two kits must be established after inspecting their available features.

## 7.2 Integration Requirements

The development process must establish:

- Compatibility between both kits.
- Shared world and level structure.
- Player controller compatibility.
- Camera compatibility with the top-down perspective.
- Character and enemy integration.
- Navigation and collision compatibility.
- Interaction between terrain, water, and cave systems.
- Persistence of world changes and gameplay state.

Existing functionality should be reused wherever possible rather than unnecessarily recreated.

# 9. UNRESOLVED DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

The following decisions must be made before detailed implementation.

1. **World topology:** Are the four civilizations connected through continuous terrain, or separated by geographical barriers?
2. **World scale:** What are the approximate dimensions of the complete world and individual civilizations?
3. **Camera:** What are the camera angle, zoom limits, and movement restrictions?
4. **Water traversal:** Which swimming, diving, and underwater interaction mechanics are required?
5. **Caves:** Are caves individual levels, connected interiors, or procedurally connected spaces?
6. **Settlements:** Are settlements fully explorable or represented as smaller environmental locations?
7. **POI interaction:** Which POIs contain encounters, loot, quests, puzzles, or environmental storytelling?
8. **Enemy behaviour:** What are the enemy AI, hostility, spawning, and respawning rules?
9. **Persistence:** Which environmental and gameplay changes must survive saving and loading?
10. **Kit integration:** Which systems are supplied by each development kit, and which require custom implementation?

- Inspect both development kits.

**Phase 4 – World Content**
- Place regional POIs.

- Add geographical exploration landmarks.

**Phase 5 – Functional Integration**
