GAME TITLE: Obol of Olympus (working title)

PITCH
Build a complete, playable top-down action RPG in the style of classic Zelda, with a Greek-mythology setting. The world is made of 4 zones connected by waterways. Each zone is an open-ended labyrinth of paths, secrets, villages, caves, and one underground boss dungeon. The core loop is: explore, find treasure and lore, collect coins, upgrade gear, beat the boss, sail to the next zone.

PRIORITY RULE
Build a fully playable vertical slice of Zone 1 first (movement, combat, one village, one cave, one boss dungeon, chests, lorebooks, coins, gear upgrades, save system). Structure the code so Zones 2-4 can be added as data-driven zone configs (tilesets, enemy tables, loot tables, boss) without rewriting systems. Keep the code modular: separate files/modules for player, combat, enemies, items, zones, UI, save.

CAMERA AND CONTROLS
- Top-down view, smooth camera follow, minimap in the corner and a full map screen that fills in as the player explores.
- Keyboard + mouse and touch/gamepad support. WASD/arrows move, mouse or arrow keys aim, attack, dodge, parry, time-slow, interact, mount, inventory, map.

PLAYER
- Appearance: the head is a black silhouette. The body is never visible; the player always wears a cloak. Helmets are cosmetic and visible: Spartan helmet, Priest Kavmika, and the Rogue's hood. Cloak and helmet colors are customizable.
- Optional offhand shield for any class (used for blocking and parrying).
- Classes (chosen at start, with a weapon switchable at any time):
  1. Warrior: sword (balanced), axe (slow, heavy, breaks shields), club (stuns).
  2. Rogue: bow (ranged, charge shot), dual daggers (fast combo, backstab bonus), slingshot (cheap ranged, can hit switches and break pots).
  3. Mage: spear (reach melee), sorcery (quick bolt shots), air spirits (slow homing bolts).
- Player-only abilities: dodge roll with brief invulnerability, parry (tight timing window that staggers enemies and reflects projectiles), and time-slow (drains a meter that refills over time). Enemies and bosses never use these.
- Stats: health (hearts), stamina, a time-slow meter. Death respawns at the last shrine and keeps the coins collected, but drops a small share of them at the spot of death to recover.

MOUNTS
- Boar (charges through breakable walls), wolf (fastest, can track hidden treasure scent), horse (balanced and carries extra loot).
- Mounts can only be summoned above ground. They are automatically dismissed in caves and dungeons.

WATERWAYS AND BOATS
- Waterways separate all 4 zones. The player needs a boat to travel between zones.
- Docks in each zone act as fast-travel hubs once discovered. There are small island secrets, sea chests, and a few sea enemies along the routes.

WORLD STRUCTURE (applies to every zone)
- Each zone is an open-ended labyrinth: branching paths, loops, shortcuts, gated areas, and hidden routes, not a linear corridor. Zones are generated from hand-authored room chunks plus a seeded layout, so no two playthroughs feel identical.
- Every zone contains: 1 main village, 3-5 gathering caves (separate cave subzones), 1 boss dungeon (underground, separate from caves), wildlife, enemy camps, shrines, and many secrets.
- Boss dungeons: entered only through a distinct underground entrance that is locked until the player has found the zone's key item. All dungeon enemies are ELEMENTAL SKELETONS that match the zone's element (the same skeleton design in different elemental variants). Dungeons have puzzles, switches, mini-bosses, and a final boss room.

ZONES

Zone 1 - Cernun Woods
- Biome: coniferous forest. Element: EARTH.
- Enemies: satyrs, centaurs. Wildlife: deer, bears.
- Village: Greek-themed (white stone, columns, olive trees, an agora).
- Boss: Medusa, in an underground dungeon full of earth skeletons. Mechanic: line-of-sight gaze attacks, so the player must use pillars as cover and the parry/reflect.

Zone 2 - Galgen's Sands
- Biome: desert. Element: FIRE.
- Enemies: Pandos (not pandas; they look like faun-like trickster nomads) and Myrmikes (ant-people warriors). Wildlife: camels, rattlesnakes.
- Village: Persia-themed (domes, bazaars, tiled arches).
- Boss: Emperor Nero Claudius Divi Claudius Caesar Augustus Germanicus, in an underground dungeon of fire skeletons.

Zone 3 - Frosthavens
- Biome: icy mountains. The map spirals inward toward a central peak, and elevation is communicated visually (layered cliffs, shading, stairs, ramps, bridges).
- Enemies: Telchynes, Cyclopes. Wildlife: wolves, yaks.
- Village: Viking-looking, but inhabited by Greek Laestrygonians (giant, longboat-and-fur style).
- Boss: the Great Manticore of Frost, in an underground dungeon of ice skeletons. Element: ICE (slippery floors, freezing).

Zone 4 - The Ruins of Olympus
- Biome: volcanic ground, a sky flooded with ghosts.
- Enemies: sirens, Automatons of Hermes. Wildlife: hellhounds, empusai.
- Village: Othrys City, capital of the Titan regime, built above the burning wasteland of Olympus.
- Boss: Hades, in an underground dungeon of death-element skeletons. Element: DEATH.

GATHERING (inside caves only)
- Gathering spots only appear after the player enters a separate cave subzone. Each activity has ONE unique minigame:
  - Fishing: timing/reaction minigame.
  - Mining: rhythm or pattern-strike minigame.
  - Hunting: stealth-and-aim minigame.
  - Cooking: ingredient-combination minigame.
- Results feed crafting, healing food buffs, and upgrade materials.

MAKING EXPLORATION FUN (important, do not skip)
The levels must not feel repetitive. Implement all of these:
1. Treasure chests in 4 visible tiers (wood, bronze, silver, gold) with different art and sound. Better tiers are hidden behind puzzles, secret walls, boss shortcuts, or mount-only paths. Some chests are guarded by mini-bosses or elite enemy camps.
2. Gear tiers, from lowest to highest: Worn, Bronze, Iron, Heroic, Mythic. Each tier has a distinct look and stat bonus. Each zone drops its own themed gear set (earth, fire, ice, death), and the higher tiers appear mainly in the later zones and in hidden places.
3. Coins: collectible currency called Obols. Obols come from enemies, pots, hidden spots, chests, and gathering. Rare Gold Drachmae are hidden coins that only drop in secrets. Each zone has a fixed number of hidden collectible coins that the player can track.
4. Gear upgrades: at each village's smith, the player spends Obols plus gathered materials to upgrade weapons, shield, cloak, and helmet through 5 levels each. Upgrades change stats and slightly change the item's visuals.
5. Lorebooks: collectible books, tablets, and scrolls scattered across each zone (at least 12 per zone), each with 2-4 short paragraphs of original lore about the zone's myths and the Titan regime. They are stored in a Journal/Codex with a completion counter, and collecting full sets unlocks rewards (a cosmetic, a gear piece, or a map reveal).
6. Variety in the labyrinth: each zone has landmark locations (a ruined temple, a shipwreck, a giant statue, a hidden grove), environmental hazards fitted to its biome, wandering elite enemies, NPC side quests, secret rooms with their own tiny puzzles, breakable walls, one-way shortcuts, and weather and day/night changes.
7. Discovery rewards: shrines that restore health and set the respawn point, map fragments that reveal nearby secrets, and key items that gate later areas (Metroidvania-style backtracking with new abilities).
8. Every cave and dungeon has at least one unique room layout and a different puzzle, so that no two feel the same. Use a set of at least 8 puzzle types (pressure plates, mirror/light beams, elemental switches, push blocks, timed doors, sequence tiles, parry-reflect targets, and boss-weakness puzzles).

UI
- Hearts and stamina bar, time-slow meter, Obol counter, equipped weapon and offhand, minimap.
- Inventory screen with gear slots and tier colors, upgrade screen at smiths, Journal/Codex for lorebooks, full map with discovered chests and secrets marked.

AUDIO/VISUAL
- Distinct color palette per zone, simple animations, screen shake and hit-pause on impacts, distinct sound cues per chest tier, a coin pickup sound, and a lore pickup sound.

SAVE SYSTEM
- Autosave at shrines and on zone changes. Save the inventory, gear levels, collected lore and coins, discovered map, and boss kills.

  NW = Zone 1 Cernun Woods     NE = Zone 2 Galgen's Sands
  SW = Zone 4 Ruins of Olympus SE = Zone 3 Frosthavens
Boat route order: Zone 1 -> Zone 2 (east channel) -> Zone 3 (south channel) -> Zone 4 (west channel).
Sea markers: island chests, 4 shipwrecks (one lorebook each), sea-serpent elite, the Charybdis Eye whirlpool (needs the Zone 3 boat upgrade).
